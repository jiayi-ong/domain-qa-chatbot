*   Variance measures the volatility of the market returns.

**4. Calculate Alpha:**

*   Alpha represents the stock's excess return above or below what is predicted by the CAPM.
*   Use the following formula: `Alpha = Average Stock Return - (Risk-Free Rate + Beta * (Average Market Return - Risk-Free Rate))`
*   The term `(Average Market Return - Risk-Free Rate)` is the market risk premium.

*   **Beta:**

*   **Alpha:**
    *   Alpha > 0: The stock has outperformed the CAPM prediction.
    *   Alpha < 0: The stock has underperformed the CAPM prediction.

**Important Considerations:**

*   The CAPM is a simplified model and has limitations.
*   The choice of time period for the historical data can affect the results.
